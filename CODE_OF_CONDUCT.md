# Code of Conduct

## The short version

We are here to learn from each other's outages. Be the kind of colleague you
would want in a post-incident review: curious, direct, and hard on systems
rather than on people.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique designs, decisions and code. Do not critique the person who wrote them.
- Assume the author had constraints you cannot see. Ask before concluding.
- Accept that people work in different stacks, scales, regions and languages.
  English is the working language here, but it is a second language for many
  contributors, including the maintainer. Correct the technical content, not the
  grammar.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about someone's experience
level, sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private information,
and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns by opening a private security advisory on any repository in the
organisation, or by contacting the maintainer through the channel listed on the
organisation profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that violate this document,
and may temporarily or permanently ban accounts for repeated or severe
violations. Enforcement decisions are explained to the person affected.
